Privacy & Your Data

Updated 21 November 2026

Create My Hospitality Newsletter is a free newsletter-design tool intended to work primarily in your browser, without requiring a user account.

Newsletter drafts, images and project files

The content you enter and the images you upload into the editor are processed in your browser. Drafts may be saved on your device using browser storage (IndexedDB or, where needed, localStorage). We do not operate a backend that receives or stores your draft newsletters or uploaded images. You can download an editable backup; you can remove locally saved data through your browser's site-data settings. Clearing browser data may permanently delete your draft if you have not exported a backup.

Website analytics

We use GoatCounter for privacy-conscious website statistics, including page views and general app actions such as loading an example, exporting a file or clicking a contact link. We do not deliberately send newsletter text, uploaded image contents, business names or contact details as analytics event values. GoatCounter processes limited technical request information in providing these statistics; see GoatCounter privacy information for details. Some browsers or extensions may block analytics.

External images and websites

If you use a remote image URL or load a sample newsletter containing externally hosted images, your browser may request those images from the host, which can receive standard connection information. Opening external links or using another AI tool is subject to that service's privacy practices. The app does not automatically send newsletter content to an AI provider.

Contact by email

If you choose to click an email contact link and send us a message, your email provider sends the information you include to us, including your email address. We use that information to respond to your enquiry. We do not automatically add you to a mailing list.

Sharing and publishing

Newsletters are not automatically published or submitted to a public showcase. The app lets you download your files for your own use. It does not send mass emails on your behalf. If you import exported HTML into another email service, that provider's policies apply.
